To use the off-balance feature, you need to:

1. **Configure the Off-Balance Accounts**:
   - Go to your chart account
   - Enable the off-balance feature on the desired accounts
   - Define the linked on-balance account where the off-balance entries will be posted once a payment is made.
2. **Asset (Off-Balance)**:
   - In the System parameters, define the asset account (off-balance) that will be used as a counterpart of the on-balance income entries.
